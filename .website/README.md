## 🦄 Piano Jumper

A unicorn walks a piano keyboard seen from the side. Moving **is** playing: crossing from one key to the next sounds that note.

Play the tune, and the rainbow above completes itself.

## 🎹 Controls

- Arrow keys, or the on-screen buttons on touch.
- Hold Space (or JUMP) and release to leap. Hold longer to go farther.
- Release with no direction held to hop in place.

## 🌈 How to Play

1. The next note is highlighted. **Blue** means you can walk there.
2. **Yellow** means it needs a jump. Watch the landing marker while you charge.
3. A repeated note is a hop in place, not a step.
4. **Black keys are obstacles, not platforms.** Land on one and you play a wrong note.

Clear at 80% accuracy. The song always finishes — a rough run just leaves the rainbow gap-toothed, and you still get to hear your take played back.

Every white key is in C major, so a fumbled note stays in tune. Only the black keys sound wrong.

## ✨ Features

- Four public domain tunes: Ode to Joy, London Bridge, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, When the Saints Go Marching In.
- Melody only, generated with oscillators. No audio files.
- The rainbow is the progress bar, the score and the pass condition in one object — one wedge per note. Its seven colours are the seven white keys of an octave.
- A replay of your performance after every song, wrong notes and all.
- Desktop and mobile.
